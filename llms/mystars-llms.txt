# MyStars.tg

> Telegram-native payment platform to buy Telegram Stars and Telegram Premium with GRAM or USDT. No App Store markup. No KYC, no signup.

MyStars.tg is a self-service crypto checkout for Telegram Stars and Telegram Premium. In-app purchases on iOS and Android route through Apple's App Store or Google Play, which take a standard 30% commission on in-app purchases — that store cut is baked into the displayed price, and buying with crypto here skips the store entirely. Every Stars pack's price is listed on the site, so the price difference is checkable rather than asserted. Payments settle on the TON blockchain (native GRAM or USDT on TON), Stars are sourced directly from Fragment.com, and the service runs as a Telegram Mini App and a standalone web app.

## Products

- [Buy Telegram Stars](https://mystars.tg/): pick Stars quantity, pay with GRAM or USDT — no App Store markup (the in-app price carries the store's cut). Per-pack prices are published on the page.
- [Telegram Premium](https://mystars.tg/premium): 3 / 6 / 12 month Premium subscriptions paid in crypto, cheaper than the Telegram App. No signup, no ID. Plan prices are published on the page beside the in-app price for the same term.
